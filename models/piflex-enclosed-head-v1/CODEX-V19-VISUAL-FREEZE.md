# PiFlex Codex V19 visual freeze

V19 is the approved visible form for the enclosed PiFlex head.

Preserve without modification:

- the original blue 10-inch screen-case perimeter and rounded outer profile;
- the recessed inner bevel around the screen assembly;
- the molded logo, writing, screw holes, and existing visible case details;
- the orange central Pi bay and six-slot rear grill;
- both orange USB ears and both FLX6 mounting brackets.

USB routing must remain concealed. Each cable enters through the top-facing
socket in its ear, runs through the hollow ear, and passes through a tunnel
under the intact blue screen-case frame into the central Pi cavity. Do not cut
an exposed channel through the blue frame.

The V20 flattened finish-skin experiment is not part of this approved form.
The immutable source remains `piflex-complete-shifted-enclosure-only-v18.glb`;
V19 is generated from it by the checked-in scripts.

Before production printing, verify the chosen female USB-A panel lead body and
cable bend radius against the tunnel and ear clearances.
